Our Story

WHERE SUNDAY
HITS DIFFERENT

The Comedy Trap Sundays started with one simple idea: Tampa deserves a comedy night that goes hard. No filler, no fluff — just raw, unfiltered laughs from the best local and touring talent in the game.

Every Sunday we transform our stage into the city's most electric comedy experience. From headliners you've seen on Netflix to Tampa's own rising stars, The Trap is where real comedy lives.

Come for the laughs. Stay for the vibes. Leave changed forever.
